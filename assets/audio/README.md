# Audio assets

## Sound effects

The WAV sound effects in `sfx/` are selected from Kenney's **Interface Sounds** pack, created by Kenney and released under CC0 1.0. They were sourced from a community mirror that provides lossless WAV conversions of the original OGG files, then copied into the app without further changes. Kenney's original license notice is included in `sfx/KENNEY_LICENSE.txt`.

Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

## Background music

`music/menu_loop.wav` is **Menu Loop** by Akikazer, downloaded from OpenGameArt and listed there under CC0. It was copied without modification.

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/menu-loop
License: CC0 (public domain dedication; attribution is optional).
